When the snakes says to eve: no what God said is not true, you shall not die, then eve finds the apple attractive. This is when the first sin happens, not later, when she actually eats. Adam and Eve knew God. And yet when the snake comes they do not trust that God told them the truth, they mistrust God. And that's why they choose to eat from the tree of knowledge: when you know you don't have to trust. The tree is good, God has planted it after all. The only thing not good is the mistrust, and the action that follows from it.
